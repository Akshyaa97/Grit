# Grit — Pause. Let's think.

**Grit** is a Chrome extension designed to help you stay intentional online. It adds a layer of mindful friction to common digital habits, helping you pause before you buy, scroll, or send.

> "Stay intentional. Catch dark patterns. Reclaim your focus."

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛒 Purchase Pause
Avoid impulsive buys. Grit detects checkout and "Add to Cart" buttons on major e-commerce sites and introduces a **10-second countdown** modal. It even detects the price to show you exactly what's at stake.

### 📱 Scroll Interrupt
Break the doomscroll. On attention-trap sites like **Reddit, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts**, Grit asks for your intent when you land. If you stay for more than 10 minutes, a gentle reminder checks if you're still on track.

### ✉️ Send Delay (Gmail)
Think before you send. Grit adds a **30-second cool-down** to the Gmail "Send" button. It also analyzes your draft for emotional language (like ALL-CAPS or excessive exclamation marks) and warns you to re-read before it goes out.

### 🕵️ Dark Pattern Detector
Spot the tricks. Grit automatically flags "shady" UI patterns like:
- **Fake Urgency:** Countdown timers and "X people viewing" alerts.
- **Confirmshaming:** Guilt-tripping language on opt-out links.
- **Pre-checked boxes:** Subscription and marketing opt-ins that are checked by default.

### 📊 Weekly Mirror
A quiet look at your digital habits. The dashboard tracks your stats over time:
- Total purchases paused (and money at stake).
- Number of scrolls interrupted.
- Emails delayed.
- Dark patterns caught.

## 🛠 Installation Guide

1.  **Download the Extension:**
    Click the green **<> Code** button at the top of this page and select **Download ZIP** (or grab the latest release from the Releases tab).
2.  **Extract the Folder:**
    Unzip the downloaded file (`grit-main.zip`) to a folder on your computer.
3.  **Open Chrome Extensions:**
    Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
4.  **Enable Developer Mode:**
    Toggle the **Developer mode** switch in the top right corner to **ON**.
5.  **Load Grit:**
    Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top left and select the unzipped `Grit` folder.
6.  **Pin for Easy Access:**
    Click the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in your toolbar, find **Grit**, and click the pin (📌) icon.

## 🔒 Privacy & Technical Details

-   **Private by Design:** All data, including your intent and usage stats, stays on your local device. Grit does not track your browsing history or send data to external servers.
-   **Manifest V3:** Built using the latest Chrome extension standards for better security and performance.
-   **Lightweight:** Grit only runs on the sites you specify and uses minimal resources.
